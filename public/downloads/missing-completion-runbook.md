# Missing Completion Troubleshooting Runbook

## Preserve the evidence
Do not rewrite source statements or manually change reporting records before the failure stage is understood.

## Follow the path
1. Experience — reproduce the action and confirm the expected event fired once.
2. Transport — inspect the request, authentication, response code, and retry behavior.
3. Storage — retrieve the statement by actor, activity, registration, and time window.
4. Contract — compare verb, object, result, context, and identity with the approved example.
5. Transformation — inspect mappings, filters, aggregation rules, and effective dates.
6. Presentation — confirm the service response and interface state match the metric definition.

## Escalation package
- Reproducible learner or test identity
- Event timestamp and registration
- Expected and received statement comparison
- Retrieval evidence
- Affected release window and population
- Owner of the next unverified handoff

## Definition of done
- New events are correct.
- Historical impact is reconciled without destroying auditability.
- Reporting is verified end to end.
- Requirements, examples, and tests are updated.
- Temporary rules have an owner and retirement date.
